House rules

Club Rules

Short list. Everyone follows it, officers included.

  1. 1
    Be respectful.No insults, harassment or targeting of other members. Disagree about cars, not people.
  2. 2
    Keep it about CSR2 and the club.Stay on topic in each board. Use Discuss for anything general.
  3. 3
    Post your own work.Use your own screenshots and tunes. Credit the member or creator when you share someone else's.
  4. 4
    Play fair.No cheating, hacks, exploits or account selling, and no promoting them here.
  5. 5
    Make tune posts useful.Include the car, tier, every setting you changed and your result.
  6. 6
    No spam or ads.No unrelated promotion, repeated posts or chain messages.
  7. 7
    Officers keep the board tidy.They may remove posts that break these rules. Send feedback below if you think a call was wrong.
Getting around

Help

How the club board works.

How do I post a tune?
Open Tunes, fill in the car, tier and the settings you used, add your quarter-mile time, then press Post tune. Use the tier buttons to filter the list.
How do I add my profile?
Open Member Profiles and fill in your in-game name, crew and favourite car. Saving again updates your entry.
When do wildcards activate?
Every day from Monday to Saturday at 7:00 PM. The percentage depends on the day: 70% on Monday and Thursday, 150% on Tuesday and Friday, 30% on Wednesday and Saturday.
I can't post anything.
You need contributor access to post. Ask a club officer to upgrade your access, then reload the page.
Who can read my feedback?
Only club officers. Other members cannot see feedback entries.
How do I join the club Discord?
Install the Discord app from the "Get the Discord app" button on the welcome section, then press "Join our Discord" and accept the invite. The join button appears once an officer has added the invite link.
How do I remove something I posted?
Press Delete on your own post, then Confirm. Officers can remove any post.
